Since 2018

From a school-gate cart to a full parlour.

Strawberry started as a single ice cream cart outside a Karnal school in 2018. Word travelled fast — today it's a full parlour, still churning every batch fresh on-site with real fruit purees and imported chocolate.

Nothing sits frozen for weeks. What we scoop today was churned this morning.
