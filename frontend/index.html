Our story

Three generations, one karahi.

Started by Late Sh. Ram Prakash in 1994 as a single counter near the grain market, Shree Mithai Bhandar now serves over 40 varieties of sweets and namkeen — still hand-stirred in small batches, every morning, by the same family.

No preservatives. No stored stock overnight. What doesn't sell by evening goes to the gaushala, not the shelf.
